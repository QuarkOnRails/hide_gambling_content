Your keywords

One entry per line. slot|slots lists variants of one word (counted once). A trailing * allows any ending: sázkař*. Spaces also match hyphens and no space: bonus buy finds “bonus-buy” and “bonusbuy”. Case and accents do not matter. Entries under “Channel name contains” match anywhere in a channel’s name or handle, also inside glued words: jackpot blocks “JackpotJudo”. “casino” and “jackpot” are built in.
